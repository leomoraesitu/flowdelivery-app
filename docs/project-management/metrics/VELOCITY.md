# FlowDelivery — Velocity

## Objective

Define how FlowDelivery tracks sprint delivery capacity using completed story points.

---

## Definition

Velocity is the number of story points completed in a sprint.

Only count work that reached `🎉 Done` and satisfies the Definition of Done.

---

## How to Measure

At the end of each sprint:

1. Review all cards moved to `🎉 Done`.
2. Confirm acceptance criteria are complete.
3. Sum completed story points.
4. Record the sprint velocity.

Do not count:

- partially completed cards
- cards still in QA
- cards without accepted delivery
- work moved to Done without validation evidence

---

## How to Use

Use velocity to:

- support sprint planning
- understand team capacity
- compare planned versus completed work
- identify overcommitment

Velocity should guide planning, not pressure delivery.

---

## Trello Usage

Relevant lists:

- `✅ Ready`
- `🚧 In Progress`
- `🧪 QA`
- `🎉 Done`

Relevant fields:

- Story Points
- Sprint
- Status

---

## Interpretation Rules

Velocity is useful over multiple sprints, not as a single-sprint judgment.

Avoid using velocity to:

- compare different teams
- reward inflated estimates
- force unrealistic sprint commitments
- ignore quality or technical debt

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Only Done cards are counted
- [ ] Story points are recorded before sprint execution
- [ ] Incomplete work is excluded
- [ ] Trend is reviewed across multiple sprints
